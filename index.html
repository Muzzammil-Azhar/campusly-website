Everything your week needs

Timetable
Your section's classes for all seven days, with your own personal slots alongside.
Assignments
Due dates from your class representatives, with progress you can track.
Notes
Share and find study material by subject, type and tag. Bookmark what you need.
Chat
Section, department and campus rooms, plus direct messages with classmates.
Tasks and goals
Plan your own study tasks next to the official work.
Verified students
New accounts are checked against your college enrolment before they see campus data.

Installing the APK

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Open Campusly and sign in with your college email or Google.
